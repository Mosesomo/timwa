# Google Indexing Guide for Timwa Fisheries

## Quick Action Steps to Get Ranked in Google

Follow these steps to ensure your site appears in Google search results:

---

## Step 1: Submit to Google Search Console

1. Go to **Google Search Console**: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Start now" and sign in with your Google account
3. Select **Domain property** and enter: `timwafisheries.com`
4. Follow the DNS verification steps (add TXT record to your domain)
5. Once verified, add the sitemap:
   - Go to Sitemaps section
   - Enter: `https://timwafisheries.com/sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"

---

## Step 2: Verify with Bing Webmaster Tools

1. Go to **Bing Webmaster Tools**: https://www.bing.com/webmasters
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account
3. Add your site: `https://timwafisheries.com`
4. Verify ownership (same DNS method)
5. Submit sitemap: `https://timwafisheries.com/sitemap.xml`

---

## Step 3: Set Up Google Analytics 4

1. Go to **Google Analytics**: https://analytics.google.com
2. Create a new property for your domain
3. Add measurement ID to your code (or let Google do it)
4. Enable "Advertiser Features" for conversion tracking
5. Link with Google Search Console for search data

---

## Step 4: Monitor Search Performance

### In Google Search Console:
- **Performance**: See your keywords, rankings, impressions, clicks
- **Coverage**: Check if all pages are indexed
- **Enhancements**: View structured data validation
- **Core Web Vitals**: Monitor page speed and performance

## Step 5: Request Indexing

### Immediate Indexing (First Time):
1. In Google Search Console
2. Go to "URL Inspection" tab
3. Paste: `https://timwafisheries.com/`
4. Click "REQUEST INDEXING"
5. Repeat for key pages:
   - /about
   - /services
   - /gallery
   - /projects
   - /contact

### For New Content:
Google will automatically crawl pages referenced in your sitemap (updated weekly).
